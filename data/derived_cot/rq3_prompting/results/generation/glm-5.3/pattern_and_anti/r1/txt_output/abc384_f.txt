Looking at this problem:

- $f(x) = x/2^{v_2(x)}$, and $f(x) = x - \sum_{k\ge1} \frac{x}{2^k}[2^k \mid x]$ since $\sum_{k=1}^{v}2^{-k} = 1-2^{-v}$.
- So Answer $= T_0 - \sum_{k\ge1} T_k/2^k$ where $T_k = \sum_{i\le j,\ 2^k\mid A_i+A_j}(A_i+A_j)$, and $T_0=(N+1)\sum A_i$.
- Since $A_i+A_j \le 2\times10^7 < 2^{25}$, only $k\le 24$ matters.

**Computing $T_k$:** With residues $r_i = A_i \bmod 2^k$, counts `cnt[r]`:
- $m_k = \sum_i A_i\cdot \text{cnt}[(-r_i)\bmod 2^k]$ (all ordered pairs),
- diagonal condition $2^k\mid 2A_i \iff r_i\in\{0,2^{k-1}\}$, giving `half` $=\sum A_i$ over those,
- then $T_k = m_k + \text{half}$ (unordered off-diagonal $= m_k - \text{half}$, plus diagonal $=2\cdot\text{half}$).

Verified by hand on sample 1 (got 5) and sample 2 (got 384). All arithmetic fits in int64 ($m_k \le 4\times10^{17} < 2^{63}$); $T_k/2^k$ is exact integer division. Implemented with numpy bincount per level: total work $\sum 2^k \approx 2^{25}$ array cells plus $24N$ per-element work — fast.
