=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- Track a global time `cur`. A plant planted at time `b` has height `cur - b` at any moment.
- Query 3 with threshold H harvests plants with `cur - b >= H`, i.e., `b <= cur - H`.
- Since plants are planted in non-decreasing time order, birth times in a list are already sorted. Just advance a head pointer from the front while `birth[head] <= cur - H`; the number advanced is the answer. Each plant is inserted once and "removed" once, so total work is O(Q).

Verified against both samples (sample 1 gives 1,1; sample 2 gives 2,2,4 — both match the trace: e.g., in sample 2, at the second harvest `c = 440237616 - 214168203 = 226069413`, popping exactly the two plants born at 226069413).

Edge case: if `cur - H < 0`, no plant (birth times are ≥ 0) is popped — handled naturally by the loop condition.
