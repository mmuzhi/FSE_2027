We need to compute $\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{j=i}^N f(i,j)$ where $f(l,r)$ is the number of distinct values in the subarray $A_l, \ldots, A_r$.

**Standard technique: contribution of each element position.**

A classic approach: for each position $k$, count the number of subarrays $(i,j)$ where $A_k$ is the *first* occurrence of its value within the subarray. Then the sum of distinct values over all subarrays equals the total count of such (position, subarray) pairs.

So the answer is $\sum_k (k - p_k)(N - k + 1)$.
