    n = int(data[0]); k = int(data[1])

    idx = 2

**Approach.** Composing the chosen K functions gives value `ΠA + Σ B_{p_j}·Π_{l<j}A_{p_l}`. For a fixed set, an exchange argument on adjacent pairs shows the optimal order sorts by key `(A-1)/B` descending (a-outer beats b-outer iff `B_b(A_a-1) ≥ B_a(A_b-1)`, i.e. `(A_a-1)/B_a ≥ (A_b-1)/B_b`; keys are a total preorder, so bubble-sort swaps never decrease the value).
