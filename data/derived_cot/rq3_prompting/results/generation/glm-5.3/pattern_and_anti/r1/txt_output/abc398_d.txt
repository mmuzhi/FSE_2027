**Approach:**

Key insight: smoke "born" at time s sits at prefix-sum position P[s] (sum of wind vectors up to time s), and at time t+0.5 it sits at P[t] − P[s]. New smoke is generated at time s exactly when P[s] has never appeared among P[0..s−1] (otherwise (0,0) already has smoke). Therefore smoke exists at (R,C) at time t+0.5 iff P[t] − (R,C) is one of the distinct prefix positions seen so far.

So: maintain a set of all prefix positions (each new position triggers a generation); for each t check membership of P[t] − (R,C). O(N) with hashing, verified against all three samples (e.g., sample 1 gives 001010, sample 3 gives 00100111111000101111).
