- Each cell (r,c) is overwritten by every iteration i with i ≤ r, i ≤ c, i ≤ N+1-r, i ≤ N+1-c, so the **last** (largest) such i determines the final color: i* = min(r, c, N+1-r, N+1-c), black `#` if odd, white `.` if even.
- Verified against all samples: N=5 center (3,3) gives i*=3 → `#` (row `#.#.#` ✓); N=11 center (6,6) gives i*=6 → `.` (✓); N=2 gives `##`/`##` (✓); N=1 gives `#`.
