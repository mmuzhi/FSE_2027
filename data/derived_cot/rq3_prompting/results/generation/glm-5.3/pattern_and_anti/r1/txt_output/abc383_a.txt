There is one humidifier in the AtCoder company office. The current time is 0, and the humidifier has no water inside.

**Approach:** Simulate directly. Keep track of the water level and previous time. At each addition, subtract the elapsed time from the water level (clamped at 0, since leaking only occurs while water remains), then add V_i. Output the level after the last addition.

**Invariant:** after processing time T_i, water = max(0, prev_water − (T_i − T_{i−1})) + V_i, with T_0 = 0 and initial water 0.

**Edge cases checked:** N=1 (just V_1), long gaps where water clamps to 0 (verified against samples 2 and 3), and the clamp logic matches sample 1 step-by-step (final answer 3, 5, 57 for the three samples respectively).

def main():
