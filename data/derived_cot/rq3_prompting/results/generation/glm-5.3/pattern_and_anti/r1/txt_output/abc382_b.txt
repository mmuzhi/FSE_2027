- **Decisive constraint**: N ≤ 100, D ≤ N. Trivially small, so direct simulation works.
- **Key insight**: Each day Takahashi eats the cookie in the rightmost '@' box. So simulating D times, each time replacing the last '@' with '.', gives the answer. Equivalently, the D rightmost cookies get eaten.
- **Plan**: Read N, D, S. Loop D times: find rightmost '@' via `rfind`, replace with '.'. Print result.

**Invariant**: After k iterations, exactly the k rightmost cookies have been removed; since S has at least D '@'s, `rfind` never returns -1.
