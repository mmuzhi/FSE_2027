Looking at this problem:

- **Key constraint**: All A_i are positive integers (1 to 10^9), N ≤ 100. The common ratio may be rational (e.g., 0.8 in sample 3), so floating-point division is risky; use integer cross-multiplication instead.
- **Approach**: A is a geometric progression iff the ratio A_{i+1}/A_i equals A_2/A_1 for every i. Since all terms are positive, this is equivalent to checking `A[i]*A[0] == A[i-1]*A[1]` for all i ≥ 1 (integer arithmetic, no overflow issues in Python).
- **Edge cases**: N=2 is always "Yes" (check passes trivially); constant sequences (ratio 1) pass correctly; decreasing sequences with rational ratios pass via cross-multiplication.

- [3,6,12,24,48]: each step satisfies A[i]·3 == A[i-1]·6 → Yes ✓
