=== ANSWER ===
**Reasoning:** The typing order (A→B→…→Z) is fixed and each key's coordinate is fixed, so the finger's path is fully determined. The answer is simply the sum of |pos(cᵢ₊₁) − pos(cᵢ)| over consecutive letters of the alphabet.

**Invariant:** position of each letter = its index in S (0-based works since only differences matter).

**Verification:** Sample 1 gives 25×1 = 25 ✓; manually summing sample 2 gives 223 ✓.

```python
S = input().strip()
ans = 0
for i in range(25):
    a = S.index(chr(ord('A') + i))
    b = S.index(chr(ord('A') + i + 1))
    ans += abs(b - a)
print(ans)
```
