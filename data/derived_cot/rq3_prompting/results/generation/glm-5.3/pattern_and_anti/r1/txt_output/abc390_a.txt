=== ANSWER ===
```python
A = list(map(int, input().split()))
target = [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
ans = "No"
for i in range(4):
    B = A[:]
    B[i], B[i + 1] = B[i + 1], B[i]
    if B == target:
        ans = "Yes"
        break
print(ans)
```
