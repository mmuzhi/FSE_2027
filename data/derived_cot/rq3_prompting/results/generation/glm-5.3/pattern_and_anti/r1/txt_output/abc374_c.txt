=== ANSWER ===
**Commit:** This is the classic partition problem — minimize max(groupA, groupB) = maximize a subset sum ≤ ⌊total/2⌋. With N ≤ 20, a guaranteed-fast approach is meet-in-the-middle: split into two halves of ≤10 elements each (1024 subset sums per side), sort one side, binary-search the best complement for each sum of the other side. ~10K operations total, trivially fast, no risk with Python time limits.

**Ledger:**
- Invariant: optimal answer = total − (max achievable subset sum ≤ ⌊total/2⌋), since for any subset with sum s > total/2 its complement gives the same max and lies below half.
- Edge cases: `half - a < 0` → skip (bisect gives idx −1); best initialized to 0 (empty subset always valid since total ≥ 2).

**Recheck on samples:**
- Sample 1: total=32, half=16, best subset sum ≤16 is 15 → 17 ✓
- Sample 2: total=2, best=1 → 1 ✓
- Sample 3: total=171, half=85, best is 82 (e.g., 25+26+31) → 89 ✓
