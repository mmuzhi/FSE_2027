Looking at this problem:

- **Key insight**: When going from shift `k` to `k+1`, every element increases by 1 except those with `B_i = M-1` (i.e. `A_i = M-1-k`), which wrap to 0. Analyzing pair contributions: non-wrapping pairs are unchanged, wrapping-wrapping pairs stay non-inversions, a wrapping element loses inversions with all non-wrapping elements after it and gains inversions with all non-wrapping elements before it. Summing over wrapping elements at 1-indexed positions (pairs among wrappers cancel out), the delta is `2·(sum of positions) - cnt·(N+1)`.

- So compute `inv(A)` once, precompute per-value count and position-sum, then accumulate deltas for `k = 1..M-1`
