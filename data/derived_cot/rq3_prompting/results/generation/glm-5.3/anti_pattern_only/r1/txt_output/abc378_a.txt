**Explanation:** Since operations on different colors are independent (two balls of different colors can never pair), the answer is simply the sum over each color of `floor(count / 2)`, where `count` is how many balls have that color. With only 4 balls, we count occurrences of each color (values 1–4) and sum the pairs.

- Sample 1 `2 1 2 1`: color 1 → 2 balls (1 pair), color 2 → 2 balls (1 pair) → total **2** ✓
- Sample 2 `4 4 4 1`: color 4 → 3 balls (1 pair), color 1 → 1 ball (0 pairs) → total **1** ✓
- Sample 3 `1 2 3 4`: all colors appear once → total **0** ✓
