Looking at this problem:

- A plant planted at cumulative time $t_i$ has height $T_{cur} - t_i$ at current time $T_{cur}$.
- A type-3 query with threshold $H$ harvests plants where $T_{cur} - t_i \geq H$, i.e., $t_i \leq T_{cur} - H$.
- Since cumulative time is non-decreasing, planting times are appended in non-decreasing order, so a sorted list works.
- Harvested plants always form a prefix of the remaining (sorted) list, so keep a `head` pointer and use `bisect_right` with `lo=head` to count/remove the prefix of planting times $\leq T_{cur} - H$.
