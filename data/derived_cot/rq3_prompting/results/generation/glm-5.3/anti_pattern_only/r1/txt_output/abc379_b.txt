=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- Teeth are divided into maximal runs of consecutive healthy (O) teeth, separated by cavities (X) which never heal.
- A strawberry can never span across a cavity, so each maximal run is independent.
- Within a run of length L: each strawberry permanently consumes K teeth, and the K teeth used by different strawberries must be pairwise disjoint intervals of length K. At most floor(L/K) such disjoint intervals fit in length L, and this is achievable by taking blocks from the left: positions 1..K, K+1..2K, etc.

So the answer is the sum of floor(L/K) over all maximal runs of O's.

Verification with samples:
- Sample 1: runs of lengths 2 and 4, K=3 → 0+1 = 1 ✓
- Sample 2: runs of lengths 1, 3, 4, K=2 → 0+1+2 = 3 ✓
- Sample 3: runs of lengths 8 and 5, K=5 → 1+1 = 2 ✓
