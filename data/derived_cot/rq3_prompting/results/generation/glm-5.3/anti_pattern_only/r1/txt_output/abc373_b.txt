=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- The keys are at fixed coordinates (position of character c in S).
- We must type A, B, C, ..., Z in that exact order, starting at A's position.
- Since the order of typing is fixed and each letter's position is fixed, the total distance is simply the sum of |pos(next letter) - pos(current letter)| over consecutive pairs A→B, B→C, ..., Y→Z.

**Verification with Sample 1:** S = "ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ", positions are 1..26 in order, so distance = 25. ✓

**Verification with Sample 2:** Computing positions of each letter in "MGJYIZDKSBHPVENFLQURTCWOAX" and summing absolute differences of consecutive alphabet letters gives 223. ✓ (I traced through: A:25, B:10, C:22, D:7, E:14, F:16, G:2, H:11, I:5, J:3, K:8, L:17, M:1, N:15, O:24, P:12, Q:18, R:20, S:9, T:21, U:19, V:13, W:23, X:26, Y:4, Z:6 — summing |diffs| = 223.)

The word "minimal" is trivially satisfied since the path is fully determined.

```python
S = input().strip()
pos = {c: i for i, c in enumerate(S)}
ans = 0
for i in range(25):
    cur = chr(ord('A') + i)
    nxt = chr(ord('A') + i + 1)
    ans += abs(pos[nxt] - pos[cur])
print(ans)
```
