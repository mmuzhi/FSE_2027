=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- Takahashi always eats the cookie from the rightmost box containing a cookie.
- This means over D days, he eats the D rightmost cookies (the D rightmost '@' characters in S).
- So the solution is to scan from the right, converting the first D '@' characters to '.', leaving everything else unchanged.

**Verification with samples:**
- Sample 1: `.@@.@`, D=2 → rightmost two '@' are at positions 5 and 3 → `.@...` ✓
- Sample 2: `@@@`, D=3 → all eaten → `...` ✓
- Sample 3: `@@@.@@.@@.`, D=4 → rightmost four '@' (positions 9, 8, 6, 5 in 1-indexed) eaten → `@@@.......` ✓

The algorithm is O(N), trivially fast for N ≤ 100.

def main():
    import sys
    data = sys.stdin.read().split()

    d = int(data[1])
    s = list(data[2])
    count = 0
