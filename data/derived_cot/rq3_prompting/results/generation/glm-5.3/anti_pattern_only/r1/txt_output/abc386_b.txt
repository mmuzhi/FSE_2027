=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- Each button press appends either a single digit or "00" to the display.
- The concatenation of all pressed buttons must exactly equal S, so we're partitioning S into segments of length 1 (any digit) or length 2 (only "00"), minimizing the number of segments.
- This gives a simple DP: `dp[i]` = min presses to build the first `i` characters, with transitions `dp[i] = dp[i-1] + 1` (single digit) and, if `S[i-2..i-1] == "00"`, `dp[i] = min(dp[i], dp[i-2] + 1)`.

Verification with samples:
- "1000000007": 8 consecutive zeros → four "00" presses → 10 - 4 = 6 ✓
- "998244353": no "00" pairs → 9 ✓
- "32000": run of 3 zeros → one pair → 5 - 1 = 4 ✓
