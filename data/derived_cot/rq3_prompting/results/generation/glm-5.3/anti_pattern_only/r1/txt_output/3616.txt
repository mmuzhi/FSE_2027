Starting at a zero position `i`, decrements **strictly alternate** between the right side (cells > i) and the left side (cells < i), because between consecutive bounces the ball sweeps over cells that are already zero (values never increase), so it always reaches the nearest positive cell on the opposite side of `i`.

- **Moving right first**: decrements go R, L, R, L, ... The process ends when a sweep finds no positive cell (ball exits the array). - **Moving left first**: symmetrically valid iff `L == R` or `L == R + 1`.
