Looking at this problem:

- A sequence is a geometric progression iff the ratio between consecutive terms is constant: A[i]/A[i-1] = A[1]/A[0] for all i.
- Since all A_i ≥ 1, no zero-division issues, and the common ratio is positive.
- To avoid floating-point errors (ratio can be fractional, e.g., 10, 8 → 0.8), use exact integer cross-multiplication: A[i] * A[0] == A[1] * A[i-1].
- Python's arbitrary precision integers mean no overflow (products up to ~10^18 are fine anyway).

Verification with samples:
- (3,6,12,24,48): checks 12·3=6·6, 24·3=6·12, 48·3=6·24 all hold → Yes
- (1,2,3): 3·1=3 ≠ 2·2=4 → No
- (10,8): N=2, only trivial check i=1 (8·10=8·10) → Yes (any two positive terms form a GP)
