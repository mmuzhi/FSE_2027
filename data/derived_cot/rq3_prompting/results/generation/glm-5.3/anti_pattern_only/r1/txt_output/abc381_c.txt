Looking at this problem:

- An 11/22 string has exactly one `/` at its center, with equal counts of `1`s before it and `2`s after it.
- So every 11/22 substring of S is centered at some `/` in S. For a `/` at position `i`, the longest candidate is determined by:
  - `a` = number of consecutive `1`s immediately before position `i`
  - `b` = number of consecutive `2`s immediately after position `i`
  - length = `2 * min(a, b) + 1`
- Answer is the max over all `/` positions. Since S contains at least one `/`, answer ≥ 1.

I verified against all three samples:
- Sample 1: `/` at index 3 has a=2, b=2 → 5 ✓
- Sample 2: `/` at index 2 has a=0, b=0 → 1 ✓
- Sample 3: `/` at index 14 has a=3, b=4 → 7 ✓

For efficiency, precompute `left[i]` (run of `1`s ending at i) and `right[i]` (run of `2`s starting at i) in O(N), then scan slashes. Total O(N), which easily handles N ≤ 2×10⁵.
