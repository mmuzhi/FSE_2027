def main():
    data = sys.stdin.read().split()
    n = int(data[0])
    s = data[1]
    # a_k = digit * k, prefix sums
    # coeff of 10^p is P[n-p] for p=0..n-1
    # P[m] for m=1..n
    P = [0] * (n + 1)

    digits = []

        carry = t // 10
    while carry:
        digits.append(carry % 10)
        carry //= 10
    digits.reverse()

Looking at this problem:

**Key insight:** Digit at position k (value d_k) appears in substrings [i,j] with i ≤ k ≤ j, at place value 10^(j-k). There are k choices for i, so its total contribution is d_k · k · Σ_{j=k}^{N} 10^(j-k) = d_k · k · R_{N-k+1} where R_m is the repunit (m ones).

So Answer = Σ_k a_k · R_{N-k+1} with a_k = d_k·k. The coefficient of 10^p in this sum is Σ_{k≤N-p} a_k = P[N-p], the prefix sum of a. These coefficients fit in small integers (≤ ~1.8×10^11), so I can normalize with carries digit-by-digit in O(N), avoiding slow big-integer arithmetic entirely.
