Looking at this problem:

- Sort elements by A. If the maximum A in S comes from sorted position j, then all of S lies within positions 0..j, position j must be in S, and the cost is A_j × (B_j + sum of the K−1 smallest B values among positions 0..j−1).
- Sweep j left to right, maintaining a max-heap (size K−1) of the smallest B values seen so far, tracking their sum. For each j ≥ K−1, evaluate the candidate and update the heap with B_j for future positions.
- K = 1 is just min(A_i × B_i).

This gives O(N log K) per test case. Verified against all three samples (42, 60, 14579).
