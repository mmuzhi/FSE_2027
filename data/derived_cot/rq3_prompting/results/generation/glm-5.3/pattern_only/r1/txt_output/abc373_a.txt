=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

**Constraints & approach:** 12 fixed strings, each of length 1–100. We just read 12 lines and count indices `i` (1-based) where `len(S_i) == i`. This is trivially O(12) — no algorithmic choice needed; the only plausible approach is a direct loop.

**Implementation ledger:**
- Invariant: `i` runs 1..12 matched against `len` of the i-th line read.
- Edge cases: trailing newline handled by `input()`; use `.strip()` for safety against stray whitespace. Indexing must be 1-based (sample 1: "september" has length 9 at position 9 → count 1; sample 2: "djiq"(4) and "qgmtwxmb"(8) → count 2 — both verified by hand).

**Recheck:** Loop reads exactly 12 lines, compares length to 1-based index, accumulates count, prints. Matches both samples.

```python
ans = 0
for i in range(1, 13):
    s = input().strip()
    if len(s) == i:
        ans += 1
print(ans)
```
