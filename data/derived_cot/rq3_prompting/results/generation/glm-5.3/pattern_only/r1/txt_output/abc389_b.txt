- Invariant: after each iteration, `f = n!`.

**Recheck:** X=6: n=1,f=1 → n=2,f=2 → n=3,f=6 → prints 3 ✓. X=2432902008176640000: iterates to n=20, f=20! → prints 20 ✓.
