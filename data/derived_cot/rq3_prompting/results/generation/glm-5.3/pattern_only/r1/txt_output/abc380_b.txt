=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- S starts with `|`, then alternates blocks of `-` (A_i of them) and `|`.
- Splitting S by `|` yields `['', '-'*A_1, '-'*A_2, ..., '-'*A_N, '']` — the first and last pieces are empty.
- So A is just the lengths of the middle pieces.

Verification with samples:
- `|---|-|----|-|-----|` → split → `['', '---', '-', '----', '-', '-----', '']` → lengths of parts[1:-1] = `[3,1,4,1,5]` ✓
- `|----------|` → `[10]` ✓
- `|-|-|-|------|` → `[1,1,1,6]` ✓

Edge case: minimal `|-|` → `['', '-', '']` → `[1]` ✓ (N ≥ 1 and A_i ≥ 1 guarantees non-empty middle parts).

```python
S = input().strip()
parts = S.split('|')
A = [len(p) for p in parts[1:-1]]
print(*A)
```
