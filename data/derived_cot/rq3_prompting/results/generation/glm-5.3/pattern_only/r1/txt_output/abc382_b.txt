def main():

=== ANSWER ===
**Commit:** Direct simulation is the right approach — N, D ≤ 100, so O(N·D) work is trivial. The invariant: each day, the rightmost remaining '@' becomes '.'; after D days, exactly the D rightmost '@' characters of S have been removed.

**Key state:** `s` as a mutable list; each day scan from the right, flip the first '@' found to '.'.

**Edge cases checked:**
- D equals total '@' count (all eaten → sample 2): works since D ≤ count('@') is guaranteed, the scan always finds a cookie.
- Sample 1 trace: day 1 eats index 4, day 2 eats index 2 → ".@..." ✓
- Sample 3 trace: eats indices 8,7,5,4 → "@@@......." ✓

def main():
