=== ANSWER ===
**Approach.** Process T character by character against fixed S. The LCS DP column vector `v[0..N]` (where `v[i] = LCS(T_prefix, S[1..i])`) is a complete state: appending character `c` gives `new_v[i] = max(new_v[i-1], v[i], v[i-1] + [S[i]==c])`. Since consecutive differences of `v` are in {0,1}, there are at most 2^N ≤ 1024 states. Characters only differ by their match-bitmask against S (at most 10 distinct letters + "not in S" class). So run a counting DP over states for M steps: at most 1024 × 11 transitions per step × 100 steps — trivial. Group states at the end by `v[N]`.

def main():

    N = int(data[0]); M = int(data[1]); S = data[2]
