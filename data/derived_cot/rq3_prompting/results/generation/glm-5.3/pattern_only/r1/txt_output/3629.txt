=== ANSWER ===
**Commit:** Length is additive over characters, so track only letter counts. One transformation = rotate the 26-vector right by one, then add the old `z` count into index 1 (`z → "ab"` contributes both `a` and `b`). O(26·t) total, trivially within limits.

**Ledger:**
- Invariant: after k steps, `cnt[i]` = number of occurrences of letter i mod 1e9+7 after k transformations.
- Transition: `z = cnt[25]; cnt = [z] + cnt[:25]; cnt[1] += z (mod)`.
- Edge: mod applied at the accumulation point (`cnt[1]`) and at the final sum; initial counts < MOD.

**Check (Example 1, "abcyy", t=2):** start `[1,1,1,0..,2@24]` → step1 `z=0` gives `[0,1,1,1,0..,2@25]` (b,c,d,z:2 ✓) → step2 `z=2` gives `[2,1,1,1,...]` then `cnt[1]=1+2=3` → sum = 2+3+1+1 = 7 ✓. Example 2: `azbk` → z=1 → `[1,1,0..,1@10]` +`cnt[1]+=1` → sum 5 ✓. Invariant and transitions agree; finalize.
