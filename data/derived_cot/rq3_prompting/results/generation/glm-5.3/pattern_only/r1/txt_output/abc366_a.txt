=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- Remaining votes R = N - T - A will be split arbitrarily between the two.
- N is odd, so no tie is possible in the final count.
- Outcome is decided iff one candidate wins in *all* scenarios:
  - Takahashi wins regardless iff T > A + R = N - T, i.e., 2T > N.
  - Aoki wins regardless iff A > T + R = N - A, i.e., 2A > N.

So answer is "Yes" iff `2*T > N` or `2*A > N`.

Verification against samples:
- N=7, T=4: 8>7 → Yes ✓
- N=99, T=12, A=48: 24≤99, 96≤99 → No ✓
- N=1, T=0, A=0: 0≤1 → No ✓

Edge cases (e.g., N=1,T=1,A=0 → Yes; N=3,T=1,A=1 → No) also behave correctly.
