For `f(N)` with `N` having `n` digits `d_0..d_{n-1}`:
- **Shorter lengths** (2 to n−1): for top digit `t`, each of the other `len−1` digits has `t` choices → `sum(t^(len-1))`.
- **Exactly n digits**: walk positions. At position 0, choosing top digit `c < d_0` gives `c^(n-1)` free completions. At position `i ≥ 1` (prefix fixed and still valid, i.e. all earlier digits `< d_0`), choosing digit `c` with `c ≤ min(d_i−1, d_0−1)` gives `d_0^(n-1-i)` completions; then continue tight only if `d_i < d_0`. If the whole number stays valid, add 1 for `N` itself.
