=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- The typing order is fixed (A, then B, ..., then Z), so the only possible movement is the direct (minimal) path between consecutive letters' coordinates on the number line.
- Answer = Σ |pos(next letter) − pos(current letter)| over the 25 consecutive alphabet pairs, where pos maps each character to its 1-indexed position in S.
- No initial distance counts since the finger starts at A's coordinate and distance is measured from pressing A to pressing Z.

Verified against both samples: sample 1 gives 25 (1→...→26), and manually computing sample 2's path sums to 223.

Invariant: `pos` uses 1-based coordinates matching the problem's coordinate system; only inter-letter moves are summed (key presses contribute nothing).

```python
S = input().strip()
pos = {c: i + 1 for i, c in enumerate(S)}
ans = sum(abs(pos[chr(ord('A') + i + 1)] - pos[chr(ord('A') + i)]) for i in range(25))
print(ans)
```
