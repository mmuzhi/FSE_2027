**Approach.** For a sequence, the score is $\prod_p (E_p+1)$ where $E_p$ is the total exponent of prime $p\in\{2,3,5,7,11,13\}$ in the product. Encode each element $a$ as the monomial $\prod_p x_p^{v_p(a)}$, so the length-$k$ contribution is $T\big((\sum_a m_a)^k\big)$ where $T(x^{\mathbf e})=\prod_p(e_p+1)$. The functional $T$ kills the ideal $\langle (x_p-1)^2\rangle$ (second difference of a linear function), so it factors through $R=\mathbb{F}[y_2,\dots,y_{13}]/\langle y_p^2\rangle$ ($y_p=x_p-1$), where $m_a=\prod_p(1+v_p(a)y_p)$ and $T$ is simply the sum of all $2^6=64$ coefficients. Then the answer is the coefficient-sum of $S+S^2+\dots+S^N$ with $S=\sum_{a=1}^M m_a$, computed by fast doubling of the geometric series ($H(2m)=H(m)(1+S^m)$) in this 64-dimensional ring, where multiplication only combines disjoint index pairs ($3^6=729$ pairs). Verified by hand on $N=1,M=7$ (16), $N=2,M=2$ (11), and $N=2,M=6$ (162, matching brute force).
