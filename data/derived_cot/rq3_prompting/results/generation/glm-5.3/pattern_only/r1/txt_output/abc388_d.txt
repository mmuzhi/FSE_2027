## Analysis

**Key observation:** Alien `i` becomes adult at time `i` and receives `k_i` gifts = number of earlier adults with ≥1 stone at that moment. After that, alien `i` only ever *loses* stones (one per future ceremony while positive). So if `d_i = A_i + k_i`, alien `i` gives a gift at time `t > i` iff `d_i ≥ t - i`, and its final count is `max(0, d_i - (N - i))`.

**Computing `k_i`:** Alien `j` (with `d_j` stones after its ceremony) has stones `max(0, d_j - (t-1-j))` just before time `t`, so it gives a gift at time `i` iff `e_j := j + d_j ≥ i`. Thus
`k_i = #{ j < i : e_j ≥ i }`.

Maintain a bucket array `B` where `B[v]` = number of inserted aliens with `e_j = v`, plus a running count `inactive` of aliens with `e_j < i`. Since `e_j` is fixed once computed, when moving from time `i` to `i+1`, exactly the aliens with `e_j = i` become inactive: `inactive += B[i]`. Then `k_i = (i-1) - inactive`. This is **O(N)** total.
