Looking at this problem:

- Trees are random recursive trees (parent of $i$ uniform over $1..i-1$). Answer = $(N-1)!\cdot\mathbb{E}[\text{dist}(u,v)]$.
- Edge $(j,P_j)$ is on path $u\to v$ iff $j \in S(u)\triangle S(v)$ where $S(x)$ = $\{x\}\cup$ ancestors.
- Known/derivable facts: $\Pr[j\text{ anc }x]=\frac1j$ for $j<x$; and via tower rule with subtree size $s_j(t)$ ($\mathbb{E}[s_j(v-1)|\mathcal F_u]=s_j(u)\frac{v-1}{u}$, giving joint $=g_j(u)/u$ with $g_j(u)=\frac{2u}{j(j+1)}$ for $u>j$), $\Pr[j\text{ anc }u \wedge j\text{ anc }v]=\frac{2}{j(j+1)}$ for $j<u<v$.
