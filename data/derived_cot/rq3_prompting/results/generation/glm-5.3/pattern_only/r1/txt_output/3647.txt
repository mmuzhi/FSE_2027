Looking at this problem:

**Target:** Minimize the number of *kept* queries such that every index `i` is covered by at least `nums[i]` kept queries; answer is `m - kept`, or `-1` if impossible.

**Approach (committed):** Classic greedy for interval multi-cover of points — sort queries by left endpoint; sweep indices left to right; keep a max-heap of right endpoints of unused queries with `l <= i`; whenever current active coverage (from already-selected queries still spanning `i`) is below `nums[i]`, greedily select available queries with the **largest right endpoint** (discarding popped ones with `r < i` as useless). If the heap exhausts before the need is met, even all queries can't cover index `i` → `-1`. Correctness follows from the standard exchange argument (greedy's pick with max `r` dominates any optimal solution's pick for all positions `>= i`, and earlier positions are already secured by previously selected intervals).

**Ledger:**
- Invariant: after index `i`, selected set covers indices `0..i` with sufficient multiplicity.
- State: heap of available right ends, `end_cnt[r]` = expiry schedule for selected queries, `active` = current coverage at `i`.
- Edge cases: discard popped queries with `r < i`; expiry via `active -= end_cnt[i-1]` when advancing.
