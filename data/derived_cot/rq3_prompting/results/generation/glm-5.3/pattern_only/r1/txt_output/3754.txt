=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- **Key invariant**: |x|+|y| = max over the four sign combos (±x ±y). For a fixed target combo (e.g., quadrant NE), each step contributes ±1 to the signed sum: +1 if the char aligns with the quadrant, −1 otherwise. A change can turn any −1 into +1 (gain of +2), and we'd never change a +1.
- **Transition**: For each of the 4 quadrant choices, track running sum `total` and count of negative contributions `neg`. At each prefix, the best achievable value with ≤ k flips is `total + 2*min(k, neg)` — achievable by flipping negatives within that prefix (each flip is a valid single character change, all within the k budget).
- **Edge cases**: k larger than the number of negatives (handled by `min`), k = 0 (reduces to plain max |x|+|y| via the four combos).

Verified against both examples: Example 1 gives 3 (combo NW or SW), Example 2 gives 6 (combo NW: prefix sums 1,0,1,2,1,2 with negatives 0,1,1,1,2,2 → last prefix 2+4=6).

Complexity: O(4n) time, O(1) extra space.

class Solution:
