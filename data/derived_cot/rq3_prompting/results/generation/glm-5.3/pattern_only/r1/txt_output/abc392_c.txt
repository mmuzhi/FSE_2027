**Verification with sample 1:** P=[4,3,2,1], Q=[2,3,1,4] (1-indexed). For j=1: ans[Q_1=2]=Q_{P_1=4}=4 ✓. For j=3: ans[Q_3=1]=Q_{P_3=2}=3 ✓. Output "3 4 1 2" matches. Also verified sample 2 fully — all 10 values match.

- Convert to 0-indexed internally, add 1 back when storing the answer.
- Read all input at once via `sys.stdin.buffer.read().split()` for speed (N up to 3×10⁵).
- Single loop assigns `res[q[j]] = q[p[j]] + 1`.
