=== ANSWER ===
**Approach:** Count frequencies with a `Counter`; a person satisfies the condition iff their value's count is 1. Scan once, tracking the max such value and its 1-based label; default to -1.

**Ledger:**
- Invariant: answer = label of the person with max A_i among those with `cnt[A_i] == 1`.
- No tie issue: the max unique value belongs to exactly one person.
- Edge cases: N=1 (trivially unique → label 1), all duplicates → -1.

**Recheck:**
- Sample 1: unique persons 4,7,8,9 with values 7,4,5,8 → max is 8 at label 9. ✓
- Sample 2: every count is 2 → prints -1. ✓
- N=1: count is 1 → prints 1. ✓

O(N) time and space, fine for N ≤ 3×10⁵.
