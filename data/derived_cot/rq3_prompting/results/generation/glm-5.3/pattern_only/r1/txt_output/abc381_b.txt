=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

**Key observations:**
- Condition 1: length must be even.
- Condition 2: characters must form adjacent equal pairs at positions (1,2), (3,4), ...
- Condition 3: each character appears exactly twice total — combined with condition 2, this means no character may appear in two different pairs (that would give count 4).

**Plan:** Check even length; iterate over pair starts; each pair must be two equal characters not seen in any earlier pair. Track seen pair-characters in a set.

**Edge cases:** length 1 (odd → No), "zzzzzz" (pairs match but 'z' repeats across pairs → No), avoid out-of-range indexing when length is odd (guarded by the initial even-length check).

Verified against all three samples:
- "aabbcc" → Yes
- "aab" (odd) → No
- "zzzzzz" (duplicate pair character) → No
