=== ANSWER ===
**Approach.** A sequence of positive numbers is a geometric progression iff every consecutive triple satisfies A_{i+1}² = A_i·A_{i+2} (this equates consecutive ratios A_{i+1}/A_i = A_{i+2}/A_{i+1} without floating point). Since A_i ≥ 1, no zero-division issue arises, and Python's big integers make the products (up to ~10¹⁸) exact.

**Ledger.**
- Invariant: check `a[i+1]*a[i+1] == a[i]*a[i+2]` for all `i` in `0..n-3`.
- Edge case: N=2 → loop empty → always Yes (matches Sample 3, ratio 0.8).
- Non-integer ratios like 3/2 (e.g., 4 6 9) correctly pass via cross-multiplication.

**Recheck.** Sample 1: 36=36, 144=144, 576=576 → Yes. Sample 2: 4≠3 → No. Sample 3: no triple → Yes. All match.

def main():
