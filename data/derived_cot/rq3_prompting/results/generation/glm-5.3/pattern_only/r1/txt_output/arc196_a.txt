**Structure of operations:** Each sequence of removals corresponds to a non‑crossing matching on positions `1..N` (each removed pair had everything between them already removed). Conversely, every non‑crossing matching with at most one unmatched element is realizable.

**Score rewrite:** For a pair `(i, j)`, `|A_i - A_j| = max - min`, contributing `+1·A` to the larger element and `-1·A` to the smaller. So the total equals `Σ c_i A_i` where exactly `⌊N/2⌋` coefficients are `+1`, exactly `⌊N/2⌋` are `-1` (and one `0` if `N` is odd).

**Upper bound:** Maximizing `Σ c_i A_i` under these count constraints means (by a simple exchange argument) assigning `+1` to the largest `⌊N/2⌋` values, `-1` to the smallest `⌊N/2⌋`, and `0` to the median (odd `N`). So:
- Even `N`: `sum(sorted[N/2:]) - sum(sorted[:N/2])`
- Odd `N`: `sum(sorted[N/2+1:]) - sum(sorted[:N/2])` (drop the median)
