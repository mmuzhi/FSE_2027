=== ANSWER ===
```python
A = list(map(int, input().split()))
target = [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

for i in range(4):
    B = A[:]
    B[i], B[i + 1] = B[i + 1], B[i]
    if B == target:
        print("Yes")
        break
else:
    print("No")
```
