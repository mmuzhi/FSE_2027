def main():
    data = sys.stdin.buffer.read().split()
    n = int(data[0])
    P = data[1:n+1]
    Q = data[n+1:2*n+1]
    # Convert to int lists

    Q = list(map(int, Q))
    # invQ[i] = person (0-indexed) wearing bib i (where bib numbers are 1-indexed, so use array of size n+1)

Looking at this problem:

- Person `j` wears bib `Q[j]` and stares at person `P[j]`.
- For bib number `i`, first find who wears it: that's person `invQ[i]` where `invQ` is the inverse permutation of `Q`.
- That person stares at person `P[invQ[i]]`, whose bib number is `Q[P[invQ[i]]]`.

So `S_i = Q[P[invQ[i]]]`.

Equivalently, define `R[j] = Q[P[j]-1]` (bib of the person person `j` stares at), then `S_i = R[invQ[i]]`.

Bib 4 worn by person 4; stares at person 1 wearing bib 2 → S₄=2 ✓. Output `3 4 1 2` matches.
