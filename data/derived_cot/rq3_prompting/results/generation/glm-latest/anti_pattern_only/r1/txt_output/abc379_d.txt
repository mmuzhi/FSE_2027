=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- Track a global time `now` (sum of all T from type-2 queries).
- A plant planted at time `t` has height `now - t` at the current time.
- A type-3 query with parameter H harvests plants with `now - t >= H`, i.e., planting time `t <= now - H`.
- Since time never goes backwards, planting times are inserted in non-decreasing order, so a simple sorted list (append-only) with a head pointer works. Each harvest removes a prefix of remaining plants; `bisect_right` finds the cut point in O(log n).

Verified against both samples (e.g., sample 2: thresholds 226069412, 226069413, 1000386391 yield counts 2, 2, 4).

import sys
from bisect import bisect_right

    idx = 1
