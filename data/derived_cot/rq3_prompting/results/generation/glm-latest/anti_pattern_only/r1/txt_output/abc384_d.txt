Looking at this problem:

- Let T = sum of one period. Prefix sums of the infinite sequence at position qN+i equal qT + B_i where B_i = A_1+...+A_i (B_0=0), for 0 ≤ i ≤ N-1.
- Any subarray sum equals dT + (B_j − B_i) with d ≥ 0, 0 ≤ i,j ≤ N−1 (since |B_j − B_i| < T, d cannot be negative). Conversely any such combination with value ≥ 1 is realizable (positivity guarantees valid ordering of endpoints).
- So achievable residues mod T are: {0} ∪ SS ∪ {T−s : s ∈ SS}, where SS = subarray sums of A[1..N−1] (indices of B are limited to [0, N−1]; subarrays ending at A_N correspond to complements, which are subarrays of A[1..N−1]).

Thus with r = S mod T:
