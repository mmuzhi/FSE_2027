=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- An 11/22 string has the form: k ones, then '/', then k twos (length 2k+1).
- Every 11/22 string has exactly one '/' at its center. So for each '/' in S at position i, find the maximum k such that k consecutive '1's immediately precede i and k consecutive '2's immediately follow i.
- Precompute `left[i]` = number of consecutive '1's ending at i, and `right[i]` = number of consecutive '2's starting at i. For a '/' at i, k = min(left[i-1], right[i+1]), giving candidate length 2k+1. Answer is the max over all '/' positions (at least 1 since S contains a '/').

Verification with samples:
- Sample 1: "211/2212", '/' at index 3: left[2]=2 ones, right[4]=2 twos → length 5. ✓
- Sample 2: "22/11", '/' at index 2: left[1]=0, right[3]=0 → length 1. ✓
- Sample 3: '/' at index 14 (string "/1211/2///2111/2222/11"): left[13]=3, right[15]=4 → k=3, length 7. ✓

The algorithm runs in O(N), well within limits for N ≤ 2×10⁵.
