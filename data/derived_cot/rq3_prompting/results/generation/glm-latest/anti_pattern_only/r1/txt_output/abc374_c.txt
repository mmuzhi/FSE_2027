=== ANSWER ===
Looking at this problem:

- We need to partition N ≤ 20 departments into two groups, minimizing the larger group total.
- With N ≤ 20, there are at most 2^20 ≈ 1M subsets. If group A gets sum `s`, group B gets `total - s`, and the cost is `max(s, total - s)`.
- Enumerate all subset sums efficiently (start with `[0]`, and for each `K_i` add `s + K_i` for all existing sums), then take the minimum of `max(s, total - s)`.

Verification with samples:
- Sample 1: total = 32; best achievable sum near 16 is 15 or 17 → answer 17. ✓
- Sample 2: total = 2; sum 1 vs 1 → answer 1. ✓
- Sample 3: total = 171; no subset sum lies strictly between 82 and 89, so 89. ✓

The empty-group case (cost = total) is never optimal since N ≥ 2, so including the empty subset in enumeration is harmless.

Complexity: O(2^N) time and memory (~1M integers), well within limits.
