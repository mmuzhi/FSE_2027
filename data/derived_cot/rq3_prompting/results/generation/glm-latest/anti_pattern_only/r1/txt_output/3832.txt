Looking at this problem:

- Swapping is a direct position exchange with **any** person: pay `cost[j]` if person j is in front of you, free if they're behind you.
- To reach position `i`, jump (paid) to the cheapest person `j ≤ i`, which puts everyone at positions `> j` behind you — then swap for free with the person at position `i`.
- Lower bound: the first time you ever reach a position `≤ i`, you must pay the person occupying that spot, and that person can only be someone originally at a position `≤ i` (nobody else could have moved there without you having been at `≤ i` already). So the cost is at least `min(cost[0..i])`.

Therefore `answer[i] = min(cost[0..i])` — the running prefix minimum. This matches both examples:
