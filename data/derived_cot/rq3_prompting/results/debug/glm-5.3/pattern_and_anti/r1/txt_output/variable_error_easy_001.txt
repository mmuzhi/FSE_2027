The bug: when recursing into the left half `[l, mid)`, the elements added to the right-side sum should be `nums[mid:r]` (i.e., `right`), not `left`. The invariant is that `lo` is the sum of everything before index `l` and `hi` is the sum of everything from index `r` onward. Verified with `nums = [2, 3, -1, 8, 4]`: the fixed recursion correctly returns index `3` (left sum `4`, right sum `4`), whereas the original returned `-1`. The right-half call `lo + left` was already consistent with this invariant, and left-to-right search order preserves the leftmost-index requirement.
