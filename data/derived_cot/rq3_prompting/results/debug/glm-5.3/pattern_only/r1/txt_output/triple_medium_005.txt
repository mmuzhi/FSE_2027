**Fixes applied:**
1. **Syntax error**: `(cur - stack[-2]` was missing a closing parenthesis.
2. **Logic bug**: After popping `cur`, the previous index on the stack is now `stack[-1]` (the pop already happened), not `stack[-2]`. The count of subarrays where `arr[cur]` is the minimum is `(i - cur) * (cur - stack[-1])`, where `i` is the right boundary (exclusive) and `stack[-1]` is the left boundary (exclusive).

**Recheck with `[3,1,2,4]`** → padded `[-inf,3,1,2,4,-inf]`:
- Sentinel `4` pops indices for `2` (contrib `2*1*1=2`), `4` (contrib `4*2*1=8`), then `1` pops `3` (contrib `3*2*1=6`); final sentinel pops `1` (contrib `1*5*1=5`). Total = 21 ✓ (matches: 3+1+1+1+2+1+2+3+4+3).
