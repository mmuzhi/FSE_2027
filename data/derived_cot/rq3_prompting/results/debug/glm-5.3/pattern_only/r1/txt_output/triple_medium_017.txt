This is the "Maximum of Absolute Value Expression" problem (LeetCode 1131). The idea is:
|arr1[i] - arr1[j]| + |arr2[i] - arr2[j]| + |i - j|

The standard solution: compute for each of the 4 sign combinations:
- i + arr1[i] + arr2[i]
- i + arr1[i] - arr2[i]
- i - arr1[i] + arr2[i]
- i - arr1[i] - arr2[i]

Then the answer is the max over all 4 of (max - min).
